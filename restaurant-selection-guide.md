These are planning ranges, not measured live routes. No itinerary selections have changed.

You do **not** need a reservation for every meal. Some casual restaurants operate queues; others offer reservations or paid priority access. Reserve your occasion meals and anything tied to a deadline. Keep arrival-night dinner flexible because your flight and immigration timing are uncertain.

How to read the tables:

- **Budget** is a broad planning allowance in yen per person, generally for food, not a verified quote. Drinks, service charges, reservation fees and upgrades can increase it. Exact published figures are identified explicitly.
- **Book** means my recommendation to reserve, not a claim that walk-ins are prohibited. **Walk-in plan** means allow waiting time; where a definitive policy was not available, reconfirm with the branch.
- Route comments are neighborhood-level judgments based on listed locations, not measured live transit journeys. Final departure times should be recalculated after you choose restaurants and confirm the go-kart operator.
- Restaurant-name links lead to the venue, official operator, booking page or cited listing. October menus, special closures and seat availability still need confirmation. I have not checked out any reservations or entered payment details.
